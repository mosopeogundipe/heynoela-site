Noela 🔒 On your phone

Privacy policy

Effective October 30, 2026

The short version

  • Noela works on your phone. There's no Noela account, and your notes, tasks and email stay on your device.
  • We don't sell your information. Noela has no ads and no tracking.
  • A few features send limited data off your phone: anonymous error codes and feature counts, flight prices (airport codes and dates only), problem reports you choose to send, weather, and Family sharing and Google Drive backups (encrypted so we can't read them). Each is explained below. Error codes, feature counts and flight prices are on unless you turn them off in Settings (in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, feature counts and flight prices start off until you turn them on); the others only happen when you use them.
  • If you connect email, Noela only reads it. It never sends, deletes or changes mail.
  • Nothing becomes a saved task until you approve it.

What Noela keeps on your phone

Everything you save stays in Noela's storage on your device:

  • Notes, tasks, dates and other items you capture or approve.
  • The original text you confirmed when saving an item, so you can always see where it came from.
  • Your settings, such as priority areas and quiet hours.
  • An activity record ("What Noela did") that lists what Noela read and what you approved. It records counts and actions, never the text of your messages.

We can't see this information, and Noela doesn't copy it to any server of ours.

Messages, photos and files you share

When you paste text, pick a photo or PDF, or share something to Noela from another app, Noela reads it on your phone. Text inside photos and PDFs is recognized on the device using Apple's or Google's built-in tools. Noela then suggests tasks and dates, and saves only the ones you approve. Photos and files are not uploaded anywhere.

Speech

Noela uses the microphone only when you tap Speak. Your speech is turned into text on your phone, where your device and language support it, and there is no cloud fallback. Audio is not saved.

On-device AI

To find tasks and dates, Noela uses the AI model built into your phone: Apple Intelligence on iPhone, or Gemini Nano on Android. The model runs on the device. If it needs to be downloaded first, Apple or Google provides the download under their own terms.

Email (Gmail and Outlook)

Connecting email is optional. If you connect a Gmail or Outlook account:

  • Read-only access. Noela asks only for permission to read mail (Gmail's gmail.readonly, Microsoft's Mail.Read). It cannot send, delete, move or label your email.
  • You choose what it reads: important mail, only specific senders, or all mail.
  • Read on your phone. Your phone downloads new messages directly from Google or Microsoft, and Noela finds tasks in them on the device. Email content is never sent to us or to anyone other than your email provider.
  • Only what you approve is saved: the task, the quoted words it came from, and a link back to the email. Noela does not keep copies of your emails.
  • Email stays on your phone. If you share a task that Noela found in your email with your household, only its title and date are shared; the quoted words, details, sender and link stay on your phone. Problem reports and error codes never include email content.
  • Sign-in is stored securely. The access that lets Noela check your mail is kept in your phone's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android).
  • You can disconnect at any time in Noela under Settings → Email. You can also remove Noela's access from your Google account or Microsoft account.

Google user data

Noela's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to show you tasks and dates you asked Noela to find. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold or transferred to anyone, it is not used to train AI models, and no person reads it.

Family sharing

Family is optional. If you create or join a household:

  • Encrypted on your phone. Items you choose to share (tasks, plans, bookings, and, only if you turn them on, calendar events and spending totals) are encrypted on your phone with a household key before they leave it. Only phones in your household hold that key.
  • What our relay sees. Shared items pass through and are stored on our relay server (run on Cloudflare) so other phones can fetch them. The relay can see a random household ID, the size and time of each encrypted item, and network details such as IP addresses that any server receives. It cannot read the contents, names or anything inside.
  • Leaving. When you leave, your phone forgets the household key. You can also choose to delete the household for everyone, which erases its encrypted data from the relay.

Weather

If you turn on weather, Noela asks Open-Meteo for the forecast. It sends either the city you typed or, if you allow approximate location, coordinates rounded to about 10 km. Noela doesn't track where you go, and we never receive your location.

If you tap Use my location for your home city, Noela uses your phone's built-in place lookup (Apple or Google) to turn your approximate position into a city name. When you type a city, Noela asks Open-Meteo for matching city names.

Travel and links

When you tap a flight or hotel search, Noela opens Google with the destination and dates in the link. What happens there is covered by Google's privacy policy. Noela doesn't book anything and gets nothing back from those sites.

Flight prices (on unless you turn it off in Settings; off until you turn it on in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland). When you tap Check prices while planning a trip, Noela sends the two airport codes and your travel dates to our server on Cloudflare, which asks FlightPowers (through RapidAPI) for current fares based on Google Flights. No name, email, trip details or anything else is sent. Our server doesn't log your searches or store IP addresses, though identical searches may be answered from a short-lived cache for up to six hours. FlightPowers and RapidAPI receive the airport codes and dates (not your IP address or anything about you) and handle them under their own privacy policies. If you open a fare, you book on Google Flights or the airline's site, under their terms.

Paperwork

Documents you keep in Paperwork (warranties, manuals, policies, receipts) and the text read from them stay in Noela's storage on your phone. Files picked from Google Drive, iCloud or another app come through your phone's own file picker; Noela gets no access to the rest of your Drive. Backups include the list of documents but not the files, and documents are never shared with your household.

Calendar

If you allow calendar access, Noela reads your phone's calendars on the device to show your day and add events you approve. Calendar data is shared only if you turn on calendar sharing in Family, and then only encrypted, as described above.

Backups

Backups are files you make and keep yourself. Each one is locked on your phone with a passphrase you choose, and we never see the file or the passphrase. If you save it to iCloud Drive, Google Drive or elsewhere, that service stores the locked file under its own terms.

If you turn on automatic backups (Plus), Noela signs in to Google Drive with permission only for its own hidden app folder (drive.appdata). It can't see or change your other Drive files. Each weekly backup is locked on your phone with your passphrase before upload, and the newest five are kept. The key that locks them is kept in your phone's secure storage so backups can run without asking; turning automatic backups off deletes it.

Noela Plus purchases

Subscriptions are bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles payment and tells the app whether your subscription is active. We don't receive your name, card or billing details.

What we collect

  • Anonymous error codes (you can turn this off in Settings). When something fails, Noela can send a short code such as "email sync timed out", with the app version, phone platform and OS version. These codes contain no content, no account details and no identifier that links them to you. They're used only to fix bugs.
  • Anonymous feature counts (you can turn this off in Settings; in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland it's off until you turn it on). Once a day, Noela can send how often features were used, such as "groceries: added 3" or "paperwork: kept 1", and which feature a chat question was about, such as "chat: groceries". It never sends what you typed or asked, or any content. Counts carry the app version and phone platform, and a random code that changes every week so counts from different weeks can't be linked. We don't store IP addresses with them. We use them only to decide what to improve.
  • Problem reports you send. If you use Report a problem, we receive what you write, the same anonymous app details, and, only if you add them, your email address and a screenshot. Please don't send screenshots that show your emails or other private details. Reports are kept in our private issue tracker (GitHub) until the problem is resolved.
  • Emails to us. If you email support or privacy, we keep the conversation to answer you.

Noela contains no advertising or tracking tools. Apple and Google also give app developers summary statistics, such as download counts, and crash reports if you've chosen to share them with developers in your phone's settings. Those come from Apple and Google under their privacy policies and don't include the contents of Noela.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it for advertising. The only other companies involved are those that run the services described above: Cloudflare (Family relay, error codes, feature counts and the flight-prices server), FlightPowers and RapidAPI (flight prices), GitHub (problem reports), Apple and Google (purchases, app distribution and, if you turn it on, Google Drive backups), and Open-Meteo (weather). We may disclose information if the law requires it, though most of what Noela handles never reaches us.

Deleting your information

  • Delete single items in the app at any time.
  • Use Settings → Erase all local data to remove all of Noela's data from your phone.
  • Deleting the app also removes its data from your phone.
  • Disconnect email in Settings → Email, or remove access from your Google or Microsoft account.
  • Leave your household in Settings → Family to remove your phone from it.
  • To delete a problem report or anything else we hold, email privacy@heynoela.com.

Children

Noela is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Your information stays on your device and benefits from your phone's own protections, such as your passcode and device encryption. Email sign-in uses each provider's official sign-in page, so Noela never sees your email password. Family items and backups are encrypted on your phone (XChaCha20-Poly1305) before they leave it.

Changes

If Noela starts handling information differently, we'll update this page and change the date above before the change takes effect.
